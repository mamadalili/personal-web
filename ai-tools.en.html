What should AI handle?
When should a person step in?

A shopper asks an assistant which product suits them. The assistant needs to know what matters to this buyer, where to find the specifications and when to hand the conversation to support. Those questions guide the customer journey, the recommendations each person sees and the job we give each agent.

THE FOCUS

AI as part of a working method

Before building a shopping assistant, for example, choose one real question and its verified answer. Then check when the assistant answers correctly and when it needs a person’s help.

  • Workflow design
  • AI-assisted production
  • Review & quality

When is this a useful fit?

  • Different AI tools give different answers about the same product, and you need to know which answer can go to a customer.
  • The team answers the same question repeatedly and could use a first draft based on verified information.
  • Content, design and analysis teams are working on the same offer and need to know what each receives from the previous step.

What can we deliver together?

A workflow map

Map a task such as receiving a buyer’s question, finding the specification, drafting an answer and referring unanswered questions to support, with an owner for each step.

Working instructions and prompts

Provide a sample input, instructions and an example of an acceptable answer for one task. The next colleague knows what to check and how to correct a mistake.

A usable example

Take a single example through the full task. We might turn a specification sheet into draft product copy and check each claim against the sheet. The example can also come from development or analysis.

Evaluate and improve the workflow

Compare answers with their sources, record recurring mistakes and costs, and document who will review future answers and how.

AI agent management

Give each agent a defined task, access to the information it needs and someone to hand the result to. Permission to draft copy and permission to publish it are separate decisions.

User-level personalization

Use the buyer’s choices and available information to shape their recommendations and content. Someone who says installation space is limited might see dimensions first.

AI-assisted customer journey design

Trace discovery, comparison, purchase and follow-up. At each step, define which question AI can answer and what sales or support needs to receive.

Chatbot and shopping assistant management

Define product-selection questions and their sources, update answers as product information changes and give buyers a route to support when the assistant can’t answer.

How does the work progress?

Choose a suitable task

Choose a recurring task that matters to the team and produces a result someone can check. Agree on which information can go into the tool before trying it.

Build and test workflows

Try a real example and one with missing information. If a product specification is unavailable, does the assistant refer the question or make up an answer?

Use it with quality control

Before everyday use, agree on what makes an answer acceptable and who corrects mistakes. The team can then tell which output is ready and which needs another review.

Before we start

What roles do AI tools and human review play?

A tool can find a specification and draft an answer. When an answer involves price, terms or a sensitive decision, a named person checks it against the authoritative source. We decide where that check belongs according to the task.

Do we need many different tools?

One tool and one reviewer may be enough to start. We add another tool when there’s a specific need and we can assess its cost, quality and upkeep.
