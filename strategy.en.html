THE FOCUS

Decisions that connect to delivery

After the review we should be able to explain, for example, why we chose to answer existing buyers’ questions first and what we’ll look at to judge that choice.

  • Market insight
  • Positioning & value
  • Growth planning

When is this a useful fit?

  • You want to grow your sales network or launch a new channel.
  • You advertise in several places and can’t tell which ones are bringing in buying enquiries.
  • Before spending on a new market, you want to know who might buy there and what evidence of demand you have.

What can we deliver together?

A market and audience picture

A summary of orders, questions and available customer information showing who may need the offer and what we still need to learn.

Positioning and a value proposition

Define whose problem the product solves, then use that answer in both the ad and the product description so buyers see a consistent promise.

A prioritised roadmap

Sequence the work around the team and the tasks that depend on each other. If an ad points to outdated delivery terms, updating the page goes on the plan before buying the ad.

A measurement framework

Agree on what to review for each choice. For a new channel, that might include how many enquiries arrive and how many are relevant to what the sales team offers.

How does the work progress?

Understand the situation

Start with one goal. For online orders, we look at how current buyers find you and which orders the team can fulfil.

Choose a direction

Compare the options with the evidence we have. If we don’t know enough about the buyer to choose between two channels, we agree on what to test before spending.

Prepare for action

Give the chosen work an owner, a sequence and a measure. After delivery we use that same measure to decide whether to continue or adjust the plan.

Before we start

What if we do not have enough data yet?

We start with the orders, questions and conversations you already have. If it’s unclear whether the intended buyer uses a new channel, a few relevant conversations or a small test can inform the decision before you commit budget.

Is strategy limited to one document?

You should be able to use the work to choose what to do first and what to postpone. When execution planning and review are part of the engagement, we follow that choice through those stages.
