# From a note to a usable post

This is an educational example, not a customer record or a product specification. It demonstrates the technique without copying the source article's prompt library.

## Material and brief

The team has received questions about whether a product fits a small space. Verified dimensions and installation clearances have not arrived. There are no sales or satisfaction measurements.

- Reader: someone checking fit before ordering.
- Question: what should I measure and what still needs confirmation?
- Purpose: help the reader ask a precise buying question.
- Available evidence: the recorded question and the stated information gap.
- Unresolved: product dimensions, required clearances and installation conditions.
- Next action: record the space's measurements and request the verified specification before deciding.

Do not turn this material into a claim that the product fits any small space, or add a sales result.

## Draft and review

Problematic wording: «بهترین انتخاب برای هر فضای کوچک؛ تجربه‌ای بی‌دردسر.»

Reviewable wording:

> قبل از سفارش، اندازهٔ محل را با مشخصات تأییدشدهٔ محصول تطبیق بده. عرض، عمق و ارتفاع را یادداشت کن. اگر در یا کشو باز می‌شود، فضای بازشدن آن را هم در نظر بگیر. شرایط نصب را از فروشنده بپرس؛ ابعاد و فاصله‌های لازم هنوز باید از تأمین‌کننده تأیید شوند.

This does not confirm fit. It gives the reader a useful check and preserves the missing evidence. Review the conditions for the actual product before using the text. Route-of-entry measurements may matter for some products, but are not a reason to invent installation requirements.

## Correction record

- Earlier wording: «مناسب هر فضای کوچک».
- Approved correction: «اندازهٔ محل را با مشخصات محصول تطبیق بده».
- Reason: the original promises suitability without the relevant specifications.
- Scope: product-fit claims in buying guidance when specifications or installation conditions are incomplete.
- Reusable rule: ask for verified dimensions and describe the matching check; do not affirm fit before those facts are available.
- Boundary: if a particular product and installation condition have been verified, explain that precise scope. The edit does not require every article or personal story to start with an instruction.

## Interaction and outcome

No numerical chart is supported by this note. A checklist is enough. A fit comparison becomes useful only after verified dimensions and relevant clearances exist; unknown values must remain explicit.

If this post is later published with authorization, inspect follow-up questions or relevant conversations over a stated window. Views alone cannot establish improved decisions or sales. Record what was actually measured and choose one question for the next attempt.
