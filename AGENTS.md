# Portfolio design and content rules

- Keep native Persian and English content and mirrored RTL/LTR layouts.
- Homepage cards are **unnumbered**. Do not add decorative ordinal labels or zero-pad display integers. Preserve meaningful source data, decimal fractions and machine-readable date formats.
- On Home, keep the welcome introduction, then “از اینجا شروع کنید” / “Find your way around”, then the existing capability hero. On desktop (1000px and above), the next scroll stage is Blog alone, full width over the pinned first view. CV and Contact belong to the later scroll stage. Use ordinary flow on phones.
- The Home blog carousel groups up to six real posts on desktop (three columns, up to two rows), four on tablets and two on phones. Preserve buttons, direction-aware keyboard input and native horizontal scrolling/swipe. Convert vertical wheel input only when the whole track is visible; tall grids must remain vertically readable. Preserve the visible post and focus on breakpoint remount; dispose listeners. Do not invent posts, repeat cards or add placeholders to fill empty slots.
- The primary accent is `#1c0998` (RGB 28, 9, 152). Keep call-to-action labels black, with light surfaces.
- Call-to-action text is bold (`700`) and black (`#000`). Use light button surfaces for readable contrast. Navigation and chart controls are distinct from calls to action.
- Keep the rounded liquid-glass navigation **fixed at the top centre**, in this order: Home, Solutions, Blog, CV, Contact. The user explicitly requested Solutions after Home. The historical element ID is `footer`; its visible placement is at the top on desktop and mobile. Keep header/anchor spacing clear of it and fit all five labels on narrow screens.
- Keep that glass bar compact: approximately 60px high on desktop and 56px on phones, roughly one fifth less than the earlier bar, while preserving at least 44px link touch targets and the liquid-glass finish.
- The homepage capability hero has four substantial, linked tiles in a balanced 2×2 grid with a 5px gap and a visible breakout beyond the hero edge. They open the dedicated Strategy, Execution, Technology and AI Tools pages under Solutions. Keep the presentation grounded in the supplied experience and explicit author statements.
- The canonical Solutions entry is `strategy.html`, with the same four-branch selector used by `execution.html`, `technology.html` and `ai-tools.html`. Home, menu and internal “Solutions” links enter that page. `solutions.html` is a noindex compatibility redirect preserving language and fragment, excluded from the sitemap. Do not restore a second overview/card hub. Centre the four bold controls with exact `#CCFF00` strokes and an obvious selected state.
- Skills and Solutions use the same bilingual collaboration CTA from `renderCollaborationCTA` in `portfolio.js`: a clear business invitation, a primary Contact action and a secondary CV action. Solutions also links to the supporting Skills page. Keep this component at the end of the hub/detail pages and preserve readable contrast, black bold action labels and mobile fit.
- Crawl metadata uses the public base in `site-config.mjs`. After adding/removing a published article or solution, run `node scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs` and `node scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs --check`. Include native fa/en variants and reciprocal alternates; omit templates, drafts, duplicate landing pages and CV view parameters. Generating files is separate from deploying them.
- Ground professional claims in the existing résumé, work history and supplied evidence. Do not invent credentials, client results, numbers or publications.
- Keep personal device paths, account/login details, credentials and private environment instructions out of public documentation and source.
- Published blog covers use separate native fa/en artwork with the topic headline inside each image. Store local WebP exports and localized alternatives in each post's `cover` metadata; `blog-covers.mjs` renders the requested language only. Show the full banner on articles and lists; keep two recent Home cards visible with compact artwork-focused thumbnails beside localized titles. Artwork is illustrative, not evidence or a photograph of the author.
- For new or revised interactive articles, read `.agents/skills/interactive-blog-anatomy/SKILL.md`, `docs/interactive-blog.md` and `prompts/interactive-blog.fa.md`. Choose interactions for the reader’s actual question. Anatomy is optional: use it only when its mechanism materially explains the subject. If it adds distraction or an unrelated physical metaphor, omit it. There is no minimum figure count and no requirement to use Anatomy in every article.
- When Anatomy is useful, load the actual `.agents/skills/anatomy/SKILL.md`, its craft reference and the closest relevant example before designing. Keep the pinned package and MIT license immutable; author figures outside the vendor directory and integrate with the existing renderer/lifecycle. Keep native fa/en, truthful models, keyboard access, reduced motion and mobile fit. The unnumbered design overrides decorative `Fig n` labels.
- Run `node scripts/check-blog-skills.mjs` and `node scripts/test-site.mjs` for blog work, plus relevant syntax/model checks and actual browser verification. The scoped runner avoids discovering the vendored skill's separate test suite. Use the host's approved UI tools; do not launch the upstream headless capture/CDP scripts as a substitute. Anatomy's optional community-gallery submission and session-log collection require their own explicit human request.

- Banner palettes come in pairs. Solo and AI Payoff keep their existing indigo pair. AI Model Market now starts the ice-white / turquoise / emerald / silver-graphite pair (`ice-emerald-pair`, position 1). The next new article must be position 2 in that same visual family, with native fa/en headlines, faint topic keywords and conceptual charts; choose a new palette only after completing the pair. Do not force the site’s purple accent into every cover or use cream/brown for this pair.

- Use `#CCFF00` as the secondary highlight: a stroke around Home’s Solutions CTA and the four Solutions controls. Keep the primary purple accent and black bold CTA text. CTA arrows are clean horizontal arrows mirrored for RTL, without tilted decorative badges.
- Shared background motion uses subtle lime particles, snow-like drift, glints and diffuse light under the content. Decorations are ARIA-hidden, ignore pointer input, pause while the document is hidden and become static with reduced motion. Preserve the existing Home hero components.
- The Blog index uses `blog-index.mjs` and `blog-library-model.mjs`: one real latest feature, a nonduplicated archive, native topic filters and bilingual keyword search. Keep article reading frames unchanged.

- The sixteen-article 2026 collection completes the ice/emerald pair with Product Pages, then follows `posts/series-2026-manifest.mjs`. Housing Offer Terms starts `silver-lemon-pair`, position 1; the next new article must complete position 2 with native fa/en artwork before starting another palette. Cover chart traces are illustrative, not evidence.

- Keep CV copy natural and concise. Do not add a calendar-system badge or explanatory label such as “تاریخ‌ها: هجری شمسی” / “Solar Hijri dates (SH)”. Preserve the actual résumé dates.
