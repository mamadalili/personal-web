# Portfolio design and content rules

- Keep native Persian and English content and mirrored RTL/LTR layouts.
- Homepage cards are **unnumbered**. Do not add decorative ordinal labels or zero-pad display integers. Preserve meaningful source data, decimal fractions and machine-readable date formats.
- On Home, keep the welcome introduction, then “از اینجا شروع کنید” / “Find your way around”, then the existing capability hero. On desktop (1000px and above), the next scroll stage is Blog alone, full width over the pinned first view. CV and Contact belong to the later scroll stage. Use ordinary flow on phones.
- The Home blog carousel groups up to six real posts on desktop (three columns, up to two rows), four on tablets and two on phones. Preserve buttons, direction-aware keyboard input and native horizontal scrolling/swipe. Convert vertical wheel input only when the whole track is visible; tall grids must remain vertically readable. Preserve the visible post and focus on breakpoint remount; dispose listeners. Do not invent posts, repeat cards or add placeholders to fill empty slots.
- The primary accent is `#1c0998` (RGB 28, 9, 152). Keep call-to-action labels black, with light surfaces.
- Call-to-action text is bold (`700`) and black (`#000`). Use light button surfaces for readable contrast. Navigation and chart controls are distinct from calls to action.
- Keep the rounded liquid-glass navigation **fixed at the top centre on desktop/tablet and bottom centre on phones (760px and below)**, in this order: Home, Solutions, Blog, CV, Contact. The user explicitly requested Solutions after Home. The historical element ID is `footer`; its visible placement is at the top on desktop/tablet and at the bottom on phones, including bottom safe-area spacing and enough content padding to keep the final actions accessible. Keep header/anchor spacing clear of it and fit all five labels on narrow screens.
- Keep that glass bar compact: approximately 60px high on desktop and 56px on phones, roughly one fifth less than the earlier bar, while preserving at least 44px link touch targets and the liquid-glass finish.
- The homepage capability hero follows the author's capsule reference: centred copy and actions above four tall, unnumbered, linked art capsules in one desktop row; two columns on phones. Use the local SVG materials in `home-hero-art.js` and the scoped `home-hero.css`, preserving the existing motion scene, portrait and approved indigo/lime accents. They open the dedicated Strategy, Execution, Technology and AI Tools pages under Solutions. Keep text readable, retain hidden-page/reduced-motion handling, and use ordinary flow on short desktop viewports when pinning would hide content. Keep the presentation grounded in supplied experience and explicit author statements.
- The canonical Solutions entry is `strategy.html`, with the same four-branch selector used by `execution.html`, `technology.html` and `ai-tools.html`. Home, menu and internal “Solutions” links enter that page. `solutions.html` is a noindex compatibility redirect preserving language and fragment, excluded from the sitemap. Do not restore a second overview/card hub. Centre the four bold controls with exact `#CCFF00` strokes and an obvious selected state.
- The four canonical Solutions pages use the same bilingual collaboration CTA from `renderCollaborationCTA` in `portfolio.js`: a clear business invitation, a primary Contact action and a secondary CV action. Its supporting work-experience link opens the CV experience tab. `skills.html` is a retired, noindex compatibility redirect to CV; do not recreate it as a separate page. Keep this component at the end of the hub/detail pages and preserve readable contrast, black bold action labels and mobile fit.
- Crawl metadata uses the public base in `site-config.mjs`. After adding/removing a published article or solution, run `node scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs` and `node scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs --check`. Include native fa/en variants and reciprocal alternates; omit templates, drafts, duplicate landing pages and CV view parameters. Generating files is separate from deploying them.
- Ground professional claims in the existing résumé, work history and supplied evidence. Do not invent credentials, client results, numbers or publications.
- The author-supplied employer logos live in `assets/companies` as lossless WebP and are referenced by the existing career records. Preserve original colors and aspect ratios on white surfaces, including selected role controls. CV and Work Stories share these career references; Contact lists the six unique supplied employers as work history, not client endorsements. Safir shares one asset across both roles; Turbin retains its letter mark until artwork is supplied. Do not generate replacement wordmarks or derive new career claims from logo artwork.
- For copy edits, follow the author's reviewed Persian voice in `docs/content-voice.fa.md`. Finalize Persian meaning first, then write native English copy with the same claims and scope. Keep interaction text tied to the reader's actual question.
- Keep personal device paths, account/login details, credentials and private environment instructions out of public documentation and source.
- Published blog covers use separate native fa/en artwork with the topic headline inside each image. Store local WebP exports and localized alternatives in each post's `cover` metadata; `blog-covers.mjs` renders the requested language only. Show the full banner on articles and lists; keep the Home carousel responsive. On phones, place each article in a single-column vertical card: full uncropped banner above title and visible excerpt, never a side thumbnail or hidden description. Two articles remain stacked per carousel group; desktop retains its six-card layout. Artwork is illustrative, not evidence or a photograph of the author.
- For new or revised interactive articles, read `.agents/skills/interactive-blog-anatomy/SKILL.md`, `docs/interactive-blog.md` and `prompts/interactive-blog.fa.md`. Choose interactions for the reader’s actual question. Anatomy is optional: use it only when its mechanism materially explains the subject. If it adds distraction or an unrelated physical metaphor, omit it. There is no minimum figure count and no requirement to use Anatomy in every article.
- When an article contains usable dated, categorical, geographic or scheduled data, also read `.agents/skills/interactive-data-visuals/SKILL.md`. Consider a Solar Hijri picker, RTL slicer, SVG map or Persian Gantt only when its required data exists and the control adds understanding. Keep this decision independent of whether Anatomy is useful.
- When Anatomy is useful, load the actual `.agents/skills/anatomy/SKILL.md`, its craft reference and the closest relevant example before designing. Keep the pinned package and MIT license immutable; author figures outside the vendor directory and integrate with the existing renderer/lifecycle. Keep native fa/en, truthful models, keyboard access, reduced motion and mobile fit. The unnumbered design overrides decorative `Fig n` labels.
- Run `node scripts/check-blog-skills.mjs` and `node scripts/test-site.mjs` for blog work, plus relevant syntax/model checks and actual browser verification. The scoped runner avoids discovering the vendored skill's separate test suite. Use the host's approved UI tools; do not launch the upstream headless capture/CDP scripts as a substitute. - Banner palettes come in pairs. Solo and AI Payoff keep their indigo pair; AI Model Market and Product Pages share the ice-white / turquoise / emerald pair. New covers should use the next open pair before starting another palette. Do not force the site’s purple accent into every cover.

- Use `#CCFF00` as the secondary highlight: a stroke around Home’s Solutions CTA and the four Solutions controls. Keep the primary purple accent and black bold CTA text. CTA arrows are clean horizontal arrows mirrored for RTL, without tilted decorative badges.
- Shared background motion uses subtle lime particles, snow-like drift, glints and diffuse light under the content. Decorations are ARIA-hidden, ignore pointer input, pause while the document is hidden and become static with reduced motion. Preserve the existing Home hero components.
- The Blog index uses `blog-index.mjs` and `blog-library-model.mjs`: one real latest feature, a nonduplicated archive, native topic filters and bilingual keyword search. Keep article reading frames unchanged.

- Keep CV copy natural and concise. Do not add a calendar-system badge or explanatory label such as “تاریخ‌ها: هجری شمسی” / “Solar Hijri dates (SH)”. Preserve the actual résumé dates.

Use the scoped `contact.css`; keep the original portrait intact and preserve reduced-motion/hidden-page handling. No invented client logos, statistics or availability claims. Phones use ordinary flow and stacked contact links.

- Home and Solutions include the shared CSS/SVG motion scene (four capability nodes, moving lime routes and monogram). Keep it decorative, responsive, paused in hidden tabs and static under reduced motion.

- Code comments only: `.agents/skills/antislop-code/SKILL.md`.

Existing author instructions and approvals take precedence over package defaults: preserve the approved palette, glass navigation, four Home capsules, portrait identity, bilingual layouts and requested motion. Do not re-request approval for an already authorized asset or change. Record the purpose of a visual technique and apply the Delivery Gate to the actual change; do not claim untouched pages were audited.

All six skills are pinned to one release in `docs/anti-slop-source.json`. Run `node scripts/check-design-skills.mjs` to verify the project copy; `--global` also checks the local Codex installation. Do not edit the upstream files or auto-update them. Keep the MIT license with the package. This package is authoring guidance and is not loaded by the website at runtime.
