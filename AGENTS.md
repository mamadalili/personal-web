# Portfolio design and content rules

- Keep native Persian and English content and mirrored RTL/LTR layouts.
- Homepage cards are **unnumbered**. Do not add decorative ordinal labels or zero-pad display integers. Preserve meaningful source data, decimal fractions and machine-readable date formats.
- On Home, keep the welcome introduction, then the “از اینجا شروع کنید” / “Find your way around” heading, then the prominent capability hero under “راهکارها” / “Solutions”. Its call to action opens the Solutions hub. On desktop (1000px and above), Blog/CV/Contact start below the first viewport and scroll over the pinned first view. On narrower screens, use ordinary document flow. Blog is the large lower card; CV and Contact are equally sized small cards.
- The primary accent is `#1c0998` (RGB 28, 9, 152). Keep call-to-action labels black, with light surfaces.
- Call-to-action text is bold (`700`) and black (`#000`). Use light button surfaces for readable contrast. Navigation and chart controls are distinct from calls to action.
- Keep the rounded liquid-glass navigation **fixed at the top centre**, in this order: Home, Solutions, Blog, CV, Contact. The user explicitly requested Solutions after Home. The historical element ID is `footer`; its visible placement is at the top on desktop and mobile. Keep header/anchor spacing clear of it and fit all five labels on narrow screens.
- The homepage capability hero has four substantial, linked tiles in a balanced 2×2 grid with a 5px gap and a visible breakout beyond the hero edge. They open the dedicated Strategy, Execution, Technology and AI Tools pages under Solutions. Keep the presentation grounded in the supplied experience and explicit author statements.
- Solutions (`solutions.html`) groups `strategy.html`, `execution.html`, `technology.html` and `ai-tools.html`. Each has native bilingual content, clear scope/outputs/process, relevant experience links and a contact action. The legacy Skills page remains available as supporting background.
- Skills and Solutions use the same bilingual collaboration CTA from `renderCollaborationCTA` in `portfolio.js`: a clear business invitation, a primary Contact action and a secondary CV action. Solutions also links to the supporting Skills page. Keep this component at the end of the hub/detail pages and preserve readable contrast, black bold action labels and mobile fit.
- Crawl metadata uses the public base in `site-config.mjs`. After adding/removing a published article or solution, run `node scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs` and `node scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs --check`. Include native fa/en variants and reciprocal alternates; omit templates, drafts, duplicate landing pages and CV view parameters. Generating files is separate from deploying them.
- Ground professional claims in the existing résumé, work history and supplied evidence. Do not invent credentials, client results, numbers or publications.
- Keep personal device paths, account/login details, credentials and private environment instructions out of public documentation and source.
- For **every new or revised interactive blog article**, load `.agents/skills/interactive-blog-anatomy/SKILL.md` and the actual `.agents/skills/anatomy/SKILL.md`, then read Anatomy's craft reference and the closest example. Also read `docs/interactive-blog.md` and use `prompts/interactive-blog.fa.md`. Include at least one substantive Anatomy figure that explains the article's concept; a decorative SVG, generic chart or merely reading the skill does not satisfy this requirement. Only an explicit user instruction changes this rule.
- Keep the pinned Anatomy package immutable, including its MIT license. Author article figures/models outside the vendor directory and integrate them with the existing static blog renderer and lifecycle. Keep native fa/en controls/readouts, truthful models, keyboard access, reduced motion and mobile fit. The user's unnumbered design overrides Anatomy's default decorative `Fig n` labels.
- Run `node scripts/check-blog-skills.mjs` and `node scripts/test-site.mjs` for blog work, plus relevant syntax/model checks and actual browser verification. The scoped runner avoids discovering the vendored skill's separate test suite. Use the host's approved UI tools; do not launch the upstream headless capture/CDP scripts as a substitute. Anatomy's optional community-gallery submission and session-log collection require their own explicit human request.
