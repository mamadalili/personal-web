Which event are we counting?

At BiNiCi I reviewed sales reports in a business intelligence dashboard to inform marketing plans. Sales engineering becomes tangible when an inquiry can be followed from first contact through quote to order.

Suppose one customer asks twice about the same product, first for a small quantity and then for a larger one. Counting both quotes as separate customers distorts the assessment of the channel that brought them in.

Agree on one inquiry

In this example, keep distinct identifiers for the customer, inquiry and quote revision. Marketing can trace the source and sales can trace the accepted quote. Both can then discuss a budget against the same piece of work.

The second quote changes only the quantity. How do you record it?

A new customer in the report Activity looks higher and the negotiation history goes missing.

A revised quote under the same inquiry It keeps the history without inventing another customer.

Pick a row and read it with sales before building the next chart. If you both recognise the same transaction, the chart has a sound starting point.
