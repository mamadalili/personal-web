How Do We Measure AI Search?

An AI answer names your shop but links to someone else’s page. What does that one answer tell you about visibility, visits and sales?

IN THIS EXPERIENCE

  • An answer can recommend your shop while linking to a magazine. Record both.
  • Keep the observed answer, recorded visit and customer order separate so each claim has a source.
  • To compare two checks, keep the buyer’s question the same and record when and where you received each answer.

Name the question before the metric

A shopper asks which shelf would fit a small kitchen. The AI answer names your shop, but the link beside it opens a magazine’s buying guide. What goes in your report? You saw the shop named and the magazine linked. You don’t yet know if anyone remembered the name, visited the shop or bought a shelf. Each event needs a different kind of evidence.

Search Engine Journal’s recap of a STAT webinar also distinguishes a brand mention from a link to a page. The STAT speaker is discussing how to measure visibility. A claim about sales at a particular shop would need the shop’s own evidence. The webinar form also discloses that registration information is shared with STAT.

Respect the boundary of Google’s data

Google’s overall Web figures in Search Console cover ordinary search and features such as AI Overviews and AI Mode. A rise in the total can’t tell you how much came from AI answers. In June 2026, Google also announced a separate report counting impressions of page URLs in its generative AI features. An impression there is different from a visit or a purchase. Inclusion in those features requires no special AI file or markup.

Web traffic rose. Which conclusion is more defensible?

Do not collapse mentions, citations and clicks

Read the shelf answer again. Looking only for your own URL would miss the recommendation. Counting the shop’s name alone wouldn’t tell you which question the magazine guide helped answer. Keep the sentence naming the shop alongside the linked page in your report. Next time, you’ll be able to see which part changed.

Add a column for events actually recorded at the shop, such as a request for the measuring guide. The buyer may already have known the brand or received the address from a friend. When there’s no checkable connection between the request and the AI answer, mark the route unknown. You can still keep the observation.

Should citation count be the main KPI?

Link counts are useful when you want to know which sources answers draw on. A name or a link alone tells you little about whether a buyer chose the shop. Ask whether the decision you intend to make with the count concerns the content, a visit or a sale.

Sample prompts that resemble real decisions

Instead of typing just “shelf,” ask the salesperson how buyers describe the problem. One rents and can’t drill the wall. Another needs room for a cabinet door to swing open. Both might use the same product word while needing very different answers.

Choose a few such questions and keep their wording for the next check. Record the date, language, location and tool too. The answer a person sees in a chat interface may differ from the one software receives through an application programming interface, or API. Changing both the wording and the tool makes differences harder to interpret.

Try five genuine pre-purchase questions and record how the shop appeared in each. Call the result what it is, a check of those five questions. Measuring the share of the whole market that saw you would require a different sample and a different denominator.

Turn citations into source investigation

If an answer recommends another shop and links to the magazine, read the guide. Perhaps it shows a clear dimensional drawing while your page offers only a photo. Correct your own product information where it’s incomplete. If the guide contains an error, you could send the publisher the measurement and its source to check.

After the change, revisit the same questions under similar conditions. Is the size described more accurately? Can buyers who reach the page find the right guide? If mentions rise while relevant enquiries stay flat, check whether those answers address your actual buyers before committing more money to promotion.

Make the limits visible in the report

Bring one observed answer, the page it linked to and the shop’s own recorded data to the next meeting. Write down what each establishes and what remains open. This week’s decision might simply be to fill in the missing product dimensions. You can justify that change without claiming AI search delivered a particular number of customers.
