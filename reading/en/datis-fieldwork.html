Listen at the counter

Part of my Datis work was deciding what needed field research and what existing sources could answer. A brief counter conversation can reveal something the final sales total can’t.

Imagine someone asking the price, hesitating between two models and leaving. “Too expensive” is an interpretation. “Asked the price, then asked how the models differ” is an observation someone can revisit.

Split the field note

Write the customer’s words on one side and your interpretation plus the next question on the other. If a second retailer reports a different pattern, you can compare observations without discarding either.

Which line belongs under observation?

“The customer couldn’t afford it” Possibly true, but still an inference without further evidence.

“What’s different about this model?” These are the words heard. A follow-up can explore the hesitation.

On the next visit, listen for one recurring question and the answer it gets. Keep the possible reason separate from the event you saw.
