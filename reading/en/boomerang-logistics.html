When is a parcel actually on its way?

From the online sales side of Boomerang, logistics starts with the promise made at checkout. The customer sees a delivery date. The shop needs to know whether the parcel is still on the packing table.

Consider a packed order with a tracking number that hasn’t been collected. A wrong address is still easy to correct. After handover the same correction has to go through the carrier.

The evidence for a change of status

Label creation, carrier collection and delivery are separate events. Give each one a checkable signal and a support colleague can answer without guessing.

What would you tell someone whose labelled parcel is still on the table?

Ready for collection It describes the current state. The handover record will justify the next update.

On its way to you The customer may call the carrier about a parcel it hasn’t received yet.

Read the tracking message as the recipient. Should you be listening for the doorbell or waiting for a dispatch update?
