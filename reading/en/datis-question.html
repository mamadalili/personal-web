A new product or a clearer explanation?

At Datis I used research to inform marketing plans and budgets for existing and new products. Suppose one model is selling slowly and the team is deciding whether to add another model or explain this one better.

Sales records show what people bought. They don’t explain why someone left. Interviews with customers cover only part of the story. Someone who considered the product but stopped may hold a different clue.

Give each answer a consequence

If people can’t distinguish the models, a comparison may be worth testing. If they understand them but need a feature neither has, the question belongs with product. Those paths call for different spending.

Whom would you speak to about an abandoned choice?

Someone who compared and walked away They can describe the last uncertainty. Check it against other cases and existing evidence.

Only repeat customers Their reasons for buying matter, but this sample misses the people who stopped.

Write down two plausible findings before funding research. If both lead to exactly the same action, sharpen the question.
