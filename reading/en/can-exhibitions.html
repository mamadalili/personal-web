The day after the exhibition

International exhibition marketing was part of my role at CAN. I like to start this story just after the stand comes down. What remains of the conversations when there’s no booth left?

Suppose two visitors leave cards. One needs installation dimensions for a model. The other wants to discuss a dealership. A list of numbers alone invites the same generic brochure to both and answers neither question.

A note that resumes the conversation

Alongside contact details shared with permission, keep the request and the next step. “Send the installation drawing for this model” is more useful than “interested prospect.” A colleague can act on it.

A visitor asks whether it fits their cabinet. What note would you keep?

Promising visitor Positive sentiment, no instruction about tomorrow’s work.

Model and request for its installation drawing The next colleague can pick up with the right document.

If every follow-up starts by asking what the visitor wanted, part of the conversation was left at the stand.
