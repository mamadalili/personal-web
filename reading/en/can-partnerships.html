From introduction to handoff

My marketing role at CAN spanned advertising and the sales network. Picture a kitchen showroom and an appliance dealer advising the same customer. It’s a useful way to examine a partnership.

One partner knows the available space. The other knows suitable models. Much of the value lies in sparing the shopper a second explanation, provided they agree to the handoff.

Put a name to the promise

In this example the first partner passes on the agreed dimensions and model. The second gives a reply window and reports the outcome. A single request is now traceable end to end.

What would you agree before printing the joint poster?

Whose logo is larger This settles a layout decision while leaving the customer’s request unclaimed.

Who answers the referral Both the shopper and the referring partner know where the next action sits.

Send one trial inquiry through the partnership. If both parties wait for the other, there’s a missing line in the agreement.
