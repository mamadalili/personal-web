After the language switch

At Boomerang my role included launching and managing international sales on a four-language site. A phrase as simple as “in stock” raises different questions depending on where the customer lives.

Consider two people reading the same English listing from different countries. The translation works for both. Shipping, order eligibility and the person handling the inquiry may differ. Language alone tells us none of those things.

An inquiry someone can answer

Suppose a buyer requests a quote. Include the model, quantity and destination and sales can address the request in the first reply. Switching languages halfway through should keep the buyer on that product.

What input do you need for a delivery estimate?

Browser language It can help choose the copy to display. It tells you nothing certain about the delivery address.

Delivery destination This lets you check service availability. The buyer can choose a correspondence language separately.

Open one product in each language, change the destination and follow it through to the first sales reply. A short form can do more for the buyer than another translated page.
