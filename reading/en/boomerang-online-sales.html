What exactly am I buying?

At Boomerang I was responsible for launching and developing online sales channels. To explain what the store has to do, I like to start where the photos end and someone has to reach for their wallet.

Imagine two versions that look identical from the front. One includes an attachment. If both share a photo and the only distinction is a long product code, buying either becomes a phone call.

Read the cart as a buyer

In this example the buyer selects the version with the attachment. That choice needs to survive the cart, the total and the receipt. Put the receipt beside the listing. Could a picker use those two pieces of information to pack the right item?

A buyer asks whether the attachment is included. Which change helps?

Show the contents of each box It answers the question where it arises. Repeat the variant name in the cart and on the receipt so the choice reaches the warehouse.

Add a discount banner A better price still leaves the buyer guessing which box is coming.

Take one item with two variants all the way to the receipt. Fix the first place where its identity disappears.
