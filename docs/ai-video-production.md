# AI Video: From Idea to Final Cut

## Editorial scope

Published 7 November 2026. Reader question: how can a small-business creator plan and inspect an AI video before paying for repeated generation? The piece follows one original gift-box example from a vague brief through shots, references, frame checks, motion instructions and editing. Persian meaning was settled first; English carries the same examples and limits.

Eian's 5 November 2026 [article](https://x.com/eianlu/status/2118317430665613741) was read in full, including embedded tables and prompts. X's public page returned 403; the public article representation exposed through FxTwitter supplied its text and entity map. The source's long prompt library was not reproduced or translated wholesale. Tool rankings, free-image assumptions, dated prices, exact model identifiers, fixed camera-angle quotas and specific post-production colour settings were not adopted as general rules. The [official Seedance guide](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/video-generation-tutorial?lang=zh) confirms the input mode discussed; compatibility still depends on model/version. This is an independent educational adaptation, not a tool benchmark or the author's reported project experience.

### Final in-body prompt

Create a landscape 3:2 language-neutral conceptual editorial illustration for an article explaining first-frame/last-frame consistency in AI video. Same ice-white, deep teal, ink and small vermilion accents as the reference. Photorealistic material / crafted 3D studio, no typography, words, logos, numbers or humans. Three large clear acrylic storyboard cards stand side by side across a pale studio surface. They depict one identical teal gift box with red ribbon, same size, color, camera viewpoint and lighting. Left: lid fully closed. Middle: lid slightly lifted vertically above its own base. Right: lid higher, above the same base. The ribbon stays clearly attached to the lid in all three images, base has NO ribbon. One precise thin vermilion motion arc near the lid suggests the intended single movement; no data charts, no floating unrelated objects. A teal pencil and a small film strip at the edge add real editing context. Beautiful depth, restrained shading, crisp readable subject, wide balanced composition.
