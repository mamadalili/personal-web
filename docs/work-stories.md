# Work stories

The page is `work-stories.html?lang=fa` / `lang=en`, linked from CV and the CV card on Home. The glass navigation continues to use its existing CV destination. All eight role records come from `career` in `portfolio.js`, including the two Safir roles and Asiatech.

`work-stories-data.mjs` retains the original 40 topic plans for compatibility with the author's content documents. The shared `visibleWorkStories` selector exposes 28: five for Boomerang, four each for Online Tahrir and CAN, three for every earlier role, including both Safir roles and Asiatech. The topic titles are editorial proposals, visibly marked as outlines. They do not establish outcomes or count as published blog articles. No case-study body, performance figure or customer evidence has been invented.

To complete a story, collect the author's account of that particular experience, examples and permitted evidence. Finalize Persian first, then native English. Follow the interactive-blog workflow where an interaction adds understanding. Add the actual article to the existing registry and set the corresponding topic's `post` to its published slug. The work-story page will then show a real reading link, using the shared article frame. Drafts, templates, missing posts and incomplete translations never receive a reading link. Native titles, excerpts, section headings and body copy (including nested controls) must be complete before a story receives that link.

Each role has a shareable `role` query. Selection supports keyboard arrows, Home/End, browser Back/Forward and native language changes. Event handlers are disposed on remount. On desktop the role list is sticky; on phones it becomes a horizontal native scroll list. The canonical bilingual page, without role-view parameters, belongs in the sitemap.

The Work stories page has one page-level CV action in its header. Role panels do not repeat that action. Both views use story-card rails beneath the employer websites, before the role's activity summary. CV preview cards open the matching role and topic in Work Stories using a stable `story` parameter and fragment. Arrival/history navigation reveals and focuses the requested card; language changes preserve focus on the switch. Outlines remain non-reading cards; publication uses the existing guard. A CV card with an article mapping uses a neutral work-story label and still leads through Work Stories. Browsing is native horizontal scroll/swipe with a visible scrollbar, and links/regions support keyboard focus. The shared glass navigation retains its CV destination.
